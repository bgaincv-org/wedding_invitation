## MyShaadhi Link — observed

[Reference invitation](https://myshaadhilink.in/invitation/vijay-rashmika-wedding-invitation?to=Guest&demo=true)

The cover combines textured yellow paper, burgundy type, a circular motif, botanical framing and temple artwork. Its button removes the cover and starts music; a persistent control mutes it. The subsequent sequence is invocation/names, couple portraits and parents, a countdown panel, ceremony itinerary with map links, photo story/gallery, attendance choices, video, and closing blessings.

The mobile composition fits 390 pixels without horizontal overflow. Portraits use ornamental frames; repeated woven borders and muggu-like patterns connect sections. The gallery opens an overlay with previous/next/close controls. Escape closes it. Choosing decline changes its pressed state; submission was deliberately not tested. A YouTube iframe is present.

Strongest patterns: textured continuity, warm family context, photographic storytelling and conspicuous venue actions. Weaknesses observed: the cover/hero uses October while the itinerary uses February, music starts with entry rather than a separate preference, and wide desktop views contain large empty stretches. Demo dates and identities are not relevant to Sathvik and Harika.

## Synthesis for this project

Keep the existing hand-painted letter identity. Enrich it with original silk/paper textures, restrained garlands, Andhra-inspired muggu, an accessible reveal, an evolving scene and a composed closing invitation. Introduce the real couple and configurable families, then give atmospheric artwork an accessible gallery until authorized photographs arrive. Maintain one date source, explicit sound control, direct access to details, and no gate requiring a ritual interaction.
