# Gallery artwork

The toranam now renders its complete image height, including leaf tips and flower pendants, and shares the gallery's motion controls. See [the seven-celebration and venue revision](CEREMONY-ASSETS.md).

Created with the built-in Imagegen tool for `wedding_invitation`. These are AI-generated photographic depictions of wedding ornaments, not photographs of family heirlooms.

- Right: `public/art/gallery/ceremonial-parasol.webp`
- Left: `public/art/gallery/jasmine-lamp.webp`
- Original transparent PNGs: `asset-sources/gallery/`
- Export: `node scripts/prepare-gallery-art.mjs` (640 × 960 WebP, alpha preserved).

The gallery continues to use this project's original wedding illustrations. No photographs from `wedding_invitation_new` are used.

## Final generation prompts

### gallery-parasol

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: transparent photographic decorative cutout for the right margin of an elegant South Indian wedding invitation website. Create one exquisitely realistic traditional Indian ceremonial wedding parasol, open ivory raw-silk canopy with deep muted maroon brocade panels, antique gold zari floral embroidery, fine gold braid, hanging gold bead tassels with tiny ivory jasmine flowers, an aged polished wooden shaft and brass finial. Full object from finial to shaft tip visible, photographed in three-quarter view tilted gently 12 degrees toward the left, convincing fabric weave and physically realistic folds, refined handcrafted wedding decor. Composition: single isolated object centered, tall portrait framing, entire silhouette safely inside frame with 8 percent clear padding, canopy at upper third and handle extending downwards. Warm diffuse natural studio light from upper left, detailed but softly lit highlights, warm muted maroon and antique gold consistent with cream wedding stationery. Transparent background with clean natural alpha edges; no floor, no environment, no person, no text, no watermark, no square backdrop, no ground shadow. Photographic realism, absolutely not a vector icon or a flat illustration.

### gallery-lamp

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: transparent photographic decorative cutout for the left margin of an elegant South Indian wedding invitation website. Create a single authentic antique South Indian brass hanging oil lamp with a slender long linked brass suspension chain, sculpted small peacock finial above a graceful shallow lotus-shaped brass oil bowl, five small softly glowing wick flames. One delicate ivory jasmine garland with a few muted ochre marigold flowers loosely wrapped near the bowl and trailing just a little below, rich handcrafted engraved brass details, warm natural patina, refined wedding ceremony decoration. Single isolated hanging object, straight-on three-quarter view, tall portrait framing, entire chain top and all petals and flowers fully visible with 8 percent clear padding. Warm diffuse natural studio light from upper left, softly lit metal, no exaggerated glow. Transparent background with clean natural alpha edges; no ceiling, no hook background, no floor, no environment, no people, no text, no watermark, no square backdrop, no ground shadow. Photographic realism with real brass and flower texture, absolutely not vector art or flat illustration.
